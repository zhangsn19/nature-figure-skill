# Final figure checklist

## Scientific correctness

- Input file, sheet, filters, exclusions, transformations, and category order are recorded.
- Experimental units and `n` are correct; technical replicates are not presented as independent samples.
- Every summary, error bar, model, and statistical annotation is defined and reproducible.
- Axis ranges, normalization, smoothing, cropping, and missing values do not distort the conclusion.
- Labels, units, group names, and legend mappings match the data and manuscript.

## Visual inspection

- The figure remains understandable at final print size and as a grayscale/color-vision-deficiency preview.
- Text, markers, lines, hatches, and error bars survive reduction without collisions.
- Panels are aligned and ordered; panel letters and references in the caption agree.
- No clipped labels, duplicate legends, excessive precision, chart junk, or unexplained abbreviations remain.

## Deliverables

- The script runs from the documented input and regenerates the outputs.
- SVG/PDF are editable; PNG is exported at 600 dpi unless the target requires otherwise.
- Fonts are embedded or use safe substitutions, and raster components meet the requested effective resolution.
- Figure notes disclose assumptions and warnings.
- For a named journal, current author instructions—not this skill—are the final authority on size, file format, color mode, and resolution.
